# Nairobi build prices: verification list

The 15 lines below are 86% of the Kenya 3-bedroom estimate (110 m², checked 21 Nov 2026). Verifying them
with dated, written quotes is what makes the calculator trustworthy and lets Quote Check open.

How to verify one line: get a written price (WhatsApp message, receipt, quote or a shop's price list) from a
Nairobi supplier, with the date, the unit (bag, kg, m, m², each, day), and whether delivery or labour is
included. Send it to Claude; Claude updates `data/build-costs.json` with the source, date and range.
